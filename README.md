# Budget Darpan

> **Budget Darpan is an AI-powered civic transparency platform that connects public budgets, government projects, procurement records, payments, official progress, and citizen evidence.**

CodeFest 2026 · Team Die Vordenker · Nepal

Budget Darpan turns fragmented local-government records into a bilingual, evidence-led experience where citizens can explore budgets, follow a project's money trail, inspect source documents, and ask questions in English, Nepali, or Romanized Nepali.

> **Safety principle:** Budget Darpan does not accuse. It identifies patterns and evidence that may require further review.

## Winning-demo strategy

The build is centered on one memorable, trustworthy story: a judge switches to Nepali, finds a real Pokhara Ward 8 Jalpa Marg road record, opens the FY 2077/78 allocation, tender, addendum, and audit citations, sees that a tender estimate is not a contract award or payment, asks where the money went, and receives an evidence-bounded answer. Later accountability phases add mock citizen verification and duplicate-safe ratings without weakening this source-first story.

This keeps the strongest differentiators connected:

- exact financial values from relational data, never generated by an LLM;
- document claims with page-level citations;
- bilingual and Romanized Nepali investigation;
- explainable anomaly indicators that never claim proven fraud;
- privacy-preserving verification and database-enforced feedback uniqueness;
- graceful offline fallbacks for the hackathon stage.

## Phase 2 verification

The evidence vertical slice now includes:

- normalized `SourceDocument`, `DocumentPage`, `DataExtractionRecord`, `DocumentChunk`, and project-document citation models with page, quality, review, and source constraints;
- a tracked evidence manifest for 25 official PDFs across Pokhara Metropolitan City and Rupa Rural Municipality, covering 3,057 pages and six fiscal years;
- SHA-256 source identity, preserved originals in ignored local media, and raw dataset PDF rules that prevent accidental Git commits;
- page-by-page text-quality scoring that detects missing text, replacement characters, fragmented glyphs, duplicated glyphs, and likely legacy Nepali fonts before choosing direct extraction or OCR;
- selective Tesseract `nep+eng` fallback using a single page render, confidence metadata, explicit warnings, and mandatory review for OCR-derived text;
- public document list, detail, page, processing-status, and project-evidence APIs with stable citation metadata;
- an English/Nepali source library and side-by-side PDF/extracted-text viewer;
- a Leaflet/OpenStreetMap project map and Recharts financial comparison connected to the Phase 1 project page;
- curated project-document links that cite the Jalpa Marg budget entry on PDF page 168, the FY 2077/78 tender and addendum, and the related audit entry on PDF page 48 without treating a probable record join as proven identity;
- separate FY 2078/79 road-upgrading and FY 2082/83 footpath procurement records with unknown allocations, preventing later packages from being silently merged into the FY 2077/78 project.

The real-data smoke test correctly detected the legacy font in `78-79.pdf`, used Nepali OCR, recorded 86.13% OCR confidence, and marked the page for review. A complete 14-page Rupa document used its clean embedded text without unnecessary OCR and was auto-accepted by the extraction quality gate.

## Real-data baseline

The default project is `PKR-W08-JALPA-2077-78` at the existing stable project UUID. It currently supports:

- an NPR 800,000 allocation reconstructed from two FY 2077/78 red-book rows—NPR 400,000 internal revenue and NPR 400,000 public participation—on PDF page 168;
- an official FY 2077/78 tender with IFB `16.1/PMC/077-78`, Contract ID `45/PMC/NCB/W/077-78`, an NPR 9,477,987.16 estimate excluding VAT and contingencies, NPR 270,000 bid security, and a 28 February 2021 submission deadline;
- an official addendum published on 22 February 2021 with a revised BOQ and contract conditions;
- an official audit citation on PDF page 48 naming Jalpa Marg drainage and blacktopping in Ward 8 and P.L. Construction within the expired/unsettled advance section;
- a visible review warning because the audit contains two NPR 690,000 ledger rows whose relationship must be checked before aggregation;
- honest unknown states for contract award, winning contractor, contract value, payments, physical progress, milestones, completion, and exact coordinates.

The budget, tender, and audit titles are similar but not identical, and the NPR 9.48 million tender estimate is much larger than the NPR 800,000 budget entry. Budget Darpan therefore presents them as a reviewable Jalpa Marg evidence cluster rather than asserting that the budget row funded the whole tender. The FY 2078/79 upgrading package and FY 2082/83 footpath package are separate projects until continuity is proven. Award, agreement, voucher, measurement-book, completion, and location evidence can be attached as the research team finds it. The Bolpatra catalogue is recorded as the official source landing page while exact opportunity-detail and direct-PDF URLs remain pending verification.

## Dependency policy

The root `requirements.txt` installs the canonical `backend/requirements.txt`. Packages are pinned for reproducible judging builds and added only with the slice that uses and tests them. Never install project packages into the global Python environment.

## Data and evidence policy

All seeded or imported financial data must be labelled as `official`, `reconstructed_from_official_sources`, `curated_demo`, or `synthetic_demo`. Missing payment information means unknown or not yet reported—it must never silently become zero. A contractor named in an audit does not become a verified contract award until the award or agreement evidence is available.

Every document-grounded answer must preserve document title, page, section where available, municipality, fiscal year, and source URL. When the evidence is insufficient, the product says so.

## Repository safety

Secrets, local databases, vector stores, virtual environments, generated assets, and every `AGENTS.md` file are ignored by Git. `AGENTS.md` is a local engineering contract and must never be added to GitHub.
